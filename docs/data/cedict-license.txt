Changes (CC BY-SA 4.0, section 3(a)(1)(B)): the glosses are modified for this app: for each word the CC-CEDICT entries that match its Taiwan reading are chosen; senses are selected and reordered (Taiwan senses first, classical ones last, 3 to 6 of them); simplified forms, pinyin and CL: lists are removed from the senses (CL: measure words are kept apart, traditional characters only); long senses and glosses are cut short with '…'; search.json lowercases them; a radical's meaning in characters.json is its character's gloss, cut to 48 characters.

Files that carry these modified glosses, shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 (their `license` field says so and credits their other sources):
  data/library-words.json: words[].en (English glosses), words[].cl (measure words from CL: lists)
  data/search.json: the last column of each row (the English gloss, lowercased)
  data/placement.json: vocab_l5[].en
  data/characters.json: radicals[].meaning
